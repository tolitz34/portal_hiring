GetJob.co · Berlin · Internship · Tech

Job Overview ✦

GetJob is an innovative software-as-a-service (SaaS) company. Our mission is to empower businesses and professionals to progress effortlessly. Our products help teams, brands, and organizations build meaningful connections and achieve their goals.

To reach millions, we need more people like you: energetic, builders, owners inside the company who are ready to grow and create. Join us to empower more businesses with technology.

We are seeking a Product Designer (UI/UX Designer) to join our team!

As a Product Designer, you will be responsible for creating intuitive and visually appealing digital products that meet the needs of our users. You will collaborate with cross-functional teams to design and deliver high-quality user experiences.

What You Will Do ✦

  • Design and develop user-friendly digital experiences.
  • Collaborate with product managers and developers.
  • Conduct research and use feedback to improve product design.
  • Maintain consistent design systems and visual standards.

Who We Are Looking For ✦

We are looking for a creative and curious designer who enjoys solving problems and turning ideas into clear, useful experiences. You communicate well, welcome feedback, and enjoy working with a collaborative team.
